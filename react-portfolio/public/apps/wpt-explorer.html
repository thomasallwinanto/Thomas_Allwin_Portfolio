VNA S-Parameter Explorer: measuring a Wireless Power Transfer link

What a Vector Network Analyzer sees when its two ports drive the Tx coil and listen on the Rx coil. S₁₁ = reflection (port 1), S₂₁ = transmission (1→2). Split peaks f_m / f_e let you MEASURE the coupling k.

k from the split: k ≈ (f_e² − f_m²)/(f_e² + f_m²). This is how you get k out of a VNA trace.

Purple = |S₂₁| (transmission, "how much gets through"). Cyan = |S₁₁| (reflection, "how much bounces back"). Orange dashed = 85 kHz drive. Grey dots mark detected peaks f_m, f_e.
Read the trace like a metrologist: one peak = under/critically coupled; two peaks = over-coupled (splitting). Deep S₁₁ dip = good match, power enters the link instead of reflecting. Increase k and watch the single resonance tear into f_m and f_e; the extracted k tracks the slider. That is the whole point of VNA characterisation.
